# Welcome!

I am an Assistant Professor of [Computer Science](https://computerscience.missouristate.edu/) at [Missouri State University](https://www.missouristate.edu/). My research spans deep learning, multivariate time-series forecasting, image restoration, and computational biology, with an emphasis on robust models for extreme events and single-cell RNA-sequencing analysis.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from the University of Nevada, Reno in 2024, advised by [Frederick C. Harris, Jr.](https://www.cse.unr.edu/~fredh/) and [Rui Wu](https://ruiwu1990.github.io/). I previously earned an M.Eng. in Control Science and Engineering from Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics and a B.Eng. in Automation from Huainan Normal University.

You can view my [curriculum vitae](/assets/CV_Yifan_Zhang.pdf), browse my [Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=d4dmtPIAAAAJ&hl=en), or contact me at [yifanzhang@missouristate.edu](mailto:yifanzhang@missouristate.edu).
